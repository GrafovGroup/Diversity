# Content and release review

This document tracks business information and editorial approvals required before a production release. Local implementation and testing can continue without hosting credentials. Do not enter passwords, private keys or other secrets in this document or in chat.

## Brand and editorial approval

- [ ] Approve the exact full-wordmark SVG/CSS execution, light/dark variants and typographic favicon against the approved Gradient Modern #9 direction.
- [ ] Confirm the descriptors: **PEOPLE | OPPORTUNITY | PROGRESS** and **GENS | OPPORTUNITÉS | PROGRÈS**.
- [ ] Review any new headline as proposed copy. “Different Perspectives. Better Outcomes.” is not a locked slogan in the master brief.
- [ ] Review both English and Canadian French for meaning, terminology and tone; have a qualified Canadian French editor review production copy.
- [ ] Confirm that the seven service offers, deliverables, delivery methods and specialist-partner responsibilities accurately reflect what the business can provide.
- [ ] Approve the original insights articles and their editorial attribution and dates. Do not present general guidance as legal advice or as evidence of client results.
- [ ] Approve the architecture photography as illustrative imagery; verify retained licence records. It does not depict company offices or completed client work.

## Verified business facts

- [ ] Confirm the registered legal entity name and any required public registration information.
- [ ] Confirm that `info@diversity.ca` exists, receives mail and is actively monitored; test inbound and outbound messages.
- [ ] Decide whether a public telephone number should appear. The brief's suggested number is not approved and should not be published without confirmation.
- [ ] Confirm the address-display policy. Do not invent local offices, teams or mailing addresses for location pages or structured data.
- [ ] Supply approved biographies, titles, credentials, headshots and consent for any people to be published. Omit individual leadership/expert profiles until verified.
- [ ] Supply verifiable accreditations, insurance information, supplier registrations, clearances and procurement identifiers if these are to be advertised.
- [ ] Approve a fact-checked capability statement before offering it as a downloadable credential document.
- [ ] Provide evidence and publication permission before adding client logos, testimonials, case studies or quantified outcomes.

## Enquiry, privacy and accessibility review

- [ ] Supply an approved form-provider endpoint through the supported environment settings and verify the recipient. Public form endpoints are configuration; provider administration credentials must stay private.
- [ ] Verify actual delivery and failure handling using the production endpoint. A successful browser request is not proof that the owner received a message.
- [ ] While the provider is unconfigured, confirm the visible email-draft fallback is acceptable. Preparing a draft does not send an enquiry; the visitor must send it in their own mail application.
- [ ] Confirm who owns lead data, who can access it, where the provider stores/processes it, how long it is retained and how deletion/access requests are handled.
- [ ] Review the privacy notice as a draft with an appropriate adviser. Do not invent retention periods, residency promises or regulatory assurances.
- [ ] Confirm the privacy contact and accessibility-feedback process.
- [ ] Review the accessibility statement after automated checks and manual keyboard, focus, screen-reader and mobile testing. Do not claim certification or comprehensive conformance on the basis of an automated scan.
- [ ] Keep sensitive attachments out of the public form until a secure transfer process is approved and implemented.
- [ ] The downloadable enquiry record is a visitor-side file for their own records; it is not a live CRM integration or evidence of a received enquiry.

## Production release gates

- [ ] Complete the actual website build, type/lint checks, route/link checks and form-flow checks. Record executed versus unrun checks in the QA report.
- [ ] Review desktop and mobile screens in both languages, including long French labels and form errors.
- [ ] Confirm the intended WHC Pro document root, access method and backup/rollback procedure in the existing deployment runbook.
- [ ] Confirm the domain's DNS provider and current web/mail records before changing anything. Preserve existing MX, SPF, DKIM and DMARC unless an approved mail migration requires changes.
- [ ] Confirm canonical host, redirects, HTTPS certificate, sitemap, robots policy and social-sharing assets.
- [ ] Obtain explicit approval of the final website and production deployment. Website implementation is not permission to change live DNS or publish the site.
- [ ] After deployment, verify public pages, forms, language switches, mail delivery, redirects and TLS. Search Console and Bing submissions require verified ownership; report them as complete only if performed.
