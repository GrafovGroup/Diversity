# Diversity.ca — Codex Build Master Brief

## Mission
Build and launch Diversity.ca as a premium, Canada-wide, bilingual (English and Canadian French) corporate and government consulting, advisory, and implementation company. Direct service provider; qualified specialist partners/subcontractors permitted, with truthful delivery disclosures. No marketplace or business directory.

## Approved brand — mandatory
- Domain and brand: **Diversity.ca**, always the complete wordmark; **no separate emblem**.
- Reference: user-approved **#9 Gradient Modern** logo, bold modern sans-serif, full wordmark in blue → purple → magenta gradient. Do not substitute teal/orange/green rainbow treatment.
- Working palette: navy #0A2D6B, blue #1967F3, lighter blue #55A6FF, purple #A855F7, pink #EC4899; refine for contrast and accessibility.
- Supporting descriptor: **PEOPLE | OPPORTUNITY | PROGRESS**; French **GENS | OPPORTUNITÉS | PROGRÈS**.
- Proposed marketing headline, pending approval: **Different Perspectives. Better Outcomes.** Do not confuse with locked descriptor.
- Professional, modern, sophisticated, inclusive, authoritative for major Canadian corporations and government. Spacious wide layouts, excellent mobile UX, authentic professional imagery, no generic stock clichés, no AI credit text.
- English/French toggle in top bar; Québec-appropriate French throughout; uppercase navigation and CTA labels.

## Seven service divisions
1. Diversity, equity and inclusion advisory: organizational assessments, policies, workforce strategy, measurable plans.
2. Accessibility advisory and audits: physical and digital accessibility assessments, compliance roadmaps, accommodations planning. Do not assert formal certifications without evidence.
3. Human resources and organizational consulting: culture, employee experience, leadership, change management.
4. Corporate training and capacity building: customized workshops, leadership training, accessibility and inclusion education.
5. Supplier diversity and procurement advisory: supplier program design, sourcing strategy, buyer/supplier engagement, measurement.
6. Social impact and sustainability consulting: stakeholder engagement, social procurement, impact planning and reporting.
7. Accessibility solutions and implementation management: product sourcing/supply, automatic door operators, signage, wayfinding, barrier removal, coordinated installation with appropriately qualified partners.

## Audiences
Equal priority: enterprise corporations and federal/provincial/municipal government, public agencies, educational institutions and other large organizations. Dedicated industry and government landing pages; procurement-ready capabilities, proposal request workflows and accessible downloadable capability statement once verified.

## Website architecture
- Home, About, Our Approach, Leadership/Experts (only verified people), Careers/Partner With Us, Contact.
- Seven division landing pages with substantive subservice pages and outcomes, methods, deliverables, FAQ, CTAs.
- Solutions for Corporations, Solutions for Government, Public Sector Procurement, Industries.
- Accessibility Products & Implementation; Request an Assessment; Request a Proposal; Training Enquiry.
- Resources/Insights with editorial quality and author/date metadata; no invented case studies or testimonials.
- Canada-wide location architecture for provinces/territories and major metros, only unique, useful pages; do not fabricate offices or local teams.
- EN and FR equivalents, language switch, hreflang/canonical, structured data appropriate to actual business, XML sitemap, robots.txt, metadata and social previews.

## Conversion and operations
- Accessible, validated lead forms with consent and anti-spam; configurable email routing, no hardcoded secret keys. Default contact info@diversity.ca subject to mailbox confirmation; default phone 833-877-8737 subject to approval.
- Intake segmentation by audience, service, geography, project timeline, budget range, procurement/RFP deadline, attachments where safely supported.
- Forms integration via configurable provider endpoint (e.g. Formspree) only after owner supplies endpoint. Do not falsely show success without submission.
- CRM-ready lead records/export, privacy and accessibility notices, compliant cookie consent only where relevant.

## Technical and delivery requirements
- First inspect repo; propose stack compatible with WHC Pro hosting. Prefer fast static or hybrid build with reliable deployment; do not assume Node server availability on shared hosting.
- Responsive accessible UI targeting WCAG 2.2 AA where practical; keyboard navigation, reduced motion, contrast-compliant gradient usage, image alt text.
- Reusable components, type-safe code where supported, design tokens, performance budgets, SEO and AEO quality, automated lint/build/test and deployment documentation.
- Provide working staging preview, run QA on desktop/mobile EN/FR, verify all routes/forms and fix broken assets.
- Prepare WHC deployment checklist and DNS instructions; never claim domain is live before DNS, SSL and public smoke test are verified.
- Publish GitHub changes via reviewed commits or PRs, preserve assets, README and CHANGELOG.

## Non-negotiables
Do not fabricate accreditations, consultants, certifications, physical offices, project results, client logos, government affiliations or legal compliance. Explicitly label proposed content and request missing credentials. Do not publish a placeholder site as finished. Do not publish until user approves production release.

## Start now
Implement Phase 0 and 1A, scaffold production-quality website architecture and produce a reviewable homepage preview. Continue phases in sequence, reporting actual completed work and blockers. Ask only for credentials, hosting access, form endpoint, and other genuinely required launch inputs.
